OUR STORY

Waterproofing is a technical discipline, not a quick fix.

Seal Kraft is positioned as a specialist waterproofing consultancy and execution partner. Our approach starts with understanding the building and the source of moisture before selecting a treatment.

We combine inspection, diagnosis, repair planning and execution oversight to reduce repeat leakage and improve the life of the structure.
